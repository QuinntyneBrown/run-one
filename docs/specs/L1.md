# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Telo Ride 1, from the Telo family, is the focused edition of Telo Ride: one class, nothing else.
The class is Intervals to the Beat. It pairs with a smart bike at home over Bluetooth, plays the
class video, or draws the Ibiza Sunset Strip course to music if the rider chooses it, and sets
the bike's resistance for every interval while the rider pedals. A ride is shaped like a race against nobody: a flyover of
the course, a countdown, the class, a finish banner, and a roll-out while the bike eases off. It
has five screens and no menus: Connect Bike, The Class, Ibiza Flyover, Class Ride and Class Done.

This document is rewritten for Telo Ride 1. It replaces Telo Ride's requirements and drops what
Telo Ride 1 leaves out: sign-in, heart rate, Home, progression, maps, races, the class library,
group classes, the custom workout, Profile, Diagnostics, feedback, the multiplayer service, World
classes, ride settings and every course but Ibiza Sunset Strip. Telo Ride's Diagnostics screen
stays left out; the diagnostic recording of L1-003 is a narrower thing, off unless switched on
(ADR 0048). A requirement kept from Telo Ride
keeps its ID, so the IDs have gaps; an ID that was removed is never used again.

These requirements are derived from the mocks in `docs/mocks/`, the source of truth for the UI.
The behaviour of existing code is not a source. The mocks draw the class, riding in Ibiza and
the shape of a ride as these requirements state them; the few criteria they do not draw yet are
listed in `L2.md`, *Mock gaps*. Each L1 is broken down in `L2.md`. The mission (`L0.md`) is not
written yet.

## L1-002: Supported bikes and browsers
Telo Ride 1 must say which bikes it works with and how well. It must work fully with the Echelon
EX-5s, EX-3, EX-8s and Connect Sport, and in beta with other bikes that speak the Bluetooth
Fitness Machine Service (FTMS). It must run in browsers that offer Web Bluetooth (Chrome and
Edge) over HTTPS. Anywhere else, it must explain why the bike cannot be reached and how to fix
it, while the class stays readable.

## L1-003: Bike connection
The app must find, pair and connect a supported bike over Bluetooth from Connect Bike, where a
new rider starts. It must remember the bike so the rider pairs it once. It must keep the
connection alive and recover from drops on its own. It must always show the rider whether the
bike is connected. When a bike will not connect, report pedalling or take a target resistance,
the app must be able to record what crossed the Bluetooth link and what it made of it, switched
on by configuration, off for every rider, and kept on the device (L1-021), so the fault can be
found.

## L1-004: Bike telemetry
The app must turn what the bike sends into live metrics: power, cadence, resistance and output,
updated about once a second. Where the bike does not report a metric (power on an Echelon), the
app must estimate it with a fixed, documented formula. A simulated bike must let every feature
run without hardware.

## L1-005: Resistance control
The app must set the bike's resistance for the rider. It follows the class's plan, so the rider
just pedals, and it lets the rider nudge the resistance by hand. It must let the rider set the
ride's intensity from 0 to 10, like a volume control, 5 being the class as it is, to make every
interval from then on harder or easier (ADR 0050). It must tell the target resistance (requested)
from the resistance (what the bike reports).

## L1-013: Class Done
After finishing the class, the rider must see the ride's output in kJ, set apart when it is a new
best, with the ride's time, its average watts, how much of the ride was on target, and its
efforts. The app must keep the rider's ride count, best output and last output on the device, so
there is a number to come back and beat. When the ride ended at an intensity other than the
rider's default, Class Done must ask whether it should be the default from then on, and keep the
answer on the device.

## L1-014: The Class
Telo Ride 1 must offer exactly one class, Intervals to the Beat by GCN Training, on its home
screen, The Class: the class video, its title and length, a chart of what the bike will do, and
one start button. A returning rider must also see their ride count, best output and last output.
The app must open on The Class for a returning rider and on Connect Bike for a new one.

## L1-015: Class Ride
The class ride must play the class video in sync with the ride clock, or draw the course when
the rider rides in Ibiza (L1-028), while the app drives the bike through the plan. It shows the current segment, its targets and the rider's power, cadence
and resistance. A countdown must lead the rider into the class. When the class ends, a finish
banner must show the ride's output, the target resistance must ease off, and the video or the
course must carry on for a few seconds before Class Done opens. The ride is always solo, so the rider must be able to pause it, leave it, or ride
it to the finish, and it must wait for the rider when the bike is lost.

## L1-021: Security and privacy
Telo Ride 1 has no accounts and must collect nothing about the rider. The rider's record must
stay on the device. Nothing may be loaded from or sent to a third party except YouTube, for the
class video and, in Ibiza, the YouTube sound the rider chooses; the course and the music are
served by the app itself. Everything that comes from a bike, from Bluetooth or from the device's storage must
be treated as untrusted data.

## L1-022: Performance and reliability
The app must stay accurate and responsive for the whole ride on a tablet or low-power laptop
beside the bike. Readouts update once a second, the clock stays true in a hidden tab, a ride
finishes without a network, and loading and memory stay within measurable budgets. The course
must be drawn within a fixed budget a low-power tablet can hold.

## L1-023: Responsive, accessible and themed UI
Every screen must work on a phone, a tablet in either orientation, a laptop and a large screen.
It must work by touch and by keyboard, meet WCAG 2.2 AA, and respect reduced motion. It is dark
by default and has a light theme. Targets must be easy to hit from the saddle.

## L1-024: Platform
The frontend must be an Angular multi-project workspace that holds the product, including all
Bluetooth work, and runs the whole ride on the device. It must be tested without real hardware or
real networks, and deployed as static files over HTTPS.

## L1-028: Riding in Ibiza
The rider must be able to ride the class in Ibiza instead of with its video: the Ibiza Sunset
Strip course, built from the class's own plan, drawn as the rider rides, with every hill arriving
as the bike's resistance changes for it. It is the only course. Before a ride in Ibiza, the Ibiza Flyover must show the rider the course from above while
it loads, and the rider must be able to skip it. While they ride in Ibiza, the rider must be able
to choose the sound: one piece of music in the background, *Beautiful Boom Bap*, the hip hop boom
bap track from Pixabay used in the marketing film; no music; a YouTube video over the course; or
that video's audio alone. The video is a default one or the class's own, in time with the ride
(ADR 0050). The ride must carry on if the course, the music or the video cannot be loaded.
