Menu

A list of actions that opens from a button.

Overview

A menu holds actions that do not earn a button of their own. It follows the WAI-ARIA Menu Button pattern.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: there are no menus. Each screen shows its few controls outright (Pause, Leave, Full screen, Music), and that is the direction: add a menu only if a screen outgrows its header, and draw it in the mocks first.

Anatomy

  1. Panel--color-panel with a hairline and shadow, 12px radius.
  2. ItemAn icon and a label, 40px tall; one item is in the tab order at a time.
  3. SeparatorA hairline between groups.
  4. Danger itemThe danger text colour, for an action that ends something.

Variants

.menu--static draws the menu in the page’s flow, for documentation; in use it is anchored to its button by .menu-anchor.

Sizes

One size.

States

StateDefaultHoverFocusDisabled
Item

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and has no breakpoints of its own.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Menu Button: the trigger has aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; the list is role="menu" with role="menuitem" children.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EnterOpens the menu and focuses the first item; activates the focused item
↓ ↑Move between items, wrapping
Home EndFirst and last item
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger

Focus

The standard focus ring: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px off the control.

Labelling

An icon-only trigger has an aria-label. A disabled item uses aria-disabled, so it can still be reached and read.

Motion

State changes fade over --duration-fast; reduced motion makes them instant.

Touch

Targets are at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • Items are verbs or nouns of one to three words, sentence case.
  • Put the action that ends something last, after a separator.
