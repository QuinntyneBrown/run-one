Component · The Class

Class video

The class video in its box: on The Class as a preview parked at 0:03, on Class Ride in sync with the clock.

Overview

The class is a YouTube video, Intervals to the Beat by GCN Training (il_OFz6o42I). It plays in one box on two screens: The Class (.the-class__video) and Class Ride (.class-video). The player has no controls of its own: the ride’s clock drives it, 0:03 ahead of the ride (the sync). In a ride in Ibiza the course takes the box instead.

The player is loaded on demand, never with the first screen. In the mocks the video’s own cover stands in for it.

Anatomy

  1. Box16:9 on --color-raised, 12px radius on Class Ride and 16px on The Class. Everything inside is clipped to it.
  2. Player or coverFills the box. The cover is YouTube’s 4:3 thumbnail; .photo--yt shows only its 16:9 picture, as the player would.
  3. OverlaysOn Class Ride the countdown, “Paused”, the finish banner and the roll-out lie inside the box.
  4. NoticeWhen the video cannot play, an inline message 8px under the box.

Variants

On The Class: 16px radius; from 1024px it takes the height left over and keeps 16:9 inside it.
On Class Ride: 12px radius.

Sizes

The box takes its size from the screen’s layout (see Responsive).

States

Paused

Paused: a 70% ink tint and “Paused”, 18px semibold. The header’s button reads Resume.

The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.

The video cannot play: the cover stays and the notice stands under it. The ride goes on (L2-088 AC4).

Responsive behaviour

  • The Class, below 1024px: full width of the column, 16:9. From 1024px: the largest 16:9 box that fits the room beside the side column (container query units).
  • Class Ride, portrait and landscape below 768px: 16:9 at the column’s width. Landscape from 768px: the box takes the height the header and the bike panel leave, never under 128px, and nothing scrolls (L2-087 AC5).

Theming

The video is a picture; only the box’s fill (--color-raised, seen while it loads) and the notice follow the theme. The paused tint is ink in both themes.

The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.

The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The player is an <iframe> titled with the class’s name; the cover is an <img> with alt="Video: Intervals to the Beat, GCN Training".

Keyboard

None in the box. Space on the header’s Pause button pauses the ride and the video together.

Focus

The player takes no focus: it has no controls. Pause, Leave and Full screen are in the header.

Labelling

The notice is role="status": it is news, but the ride does not stop for it.

Contrast

light theme
“Paused”: the page’s text on a 70% tint of the page colour (6.7:1 over the brightest frame; shown here on the solid page colour)
Notice text
Notice icon
dark theme
“Paused”: the page’s text on a 70% tint of the page colour (6.7:1 over the brightest frame; shown here on the solid page colour)
Notice text
Notice icon

Motion

The video is the motion. Pausing shows the tint without a transition.

Touch

Nothing in the box is pressed.

Content

  • “Paused”. One word.
  • “The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.” Say what is lost, then what still works.
  • The cover’s alt names the class and the channel.
