Component · Diagnostic recording

Recording mark

The one sign that the diagnostic recording is on, and the one way in to it: a button that reads “Recording”, fixed at the foot of every screen at the left.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDiagnostic aid · off for every rider

Overview

The diagnostic recording (L2-178, ADR 0048) is a diagnostic aid, not part of the rider’s design. It is on only when the app is opened at /?diagnose, and then every screen shows the recording mark. A person whose bike’s name and bytes are being held is owed that sign, and the mark is also the only control the recording puts on a screen: pressing it opens the Diagnostic recording dialog.

  • With the recording off the mark is not in the page at all: no screen differs by a pixel.
  • It adds no screen, route or menu. It is not a toggle: the recording cannot be stopped from it.
  • It is not the old .diagnostics dialog of Telo Ride, which ADR 0040 removed; this system no longer documents it.

Anatomy

  1. Button.diagnostic-mark: a pill 44px tall on the panel colour, with a hairline edge and --shadow-3, so it reads over a video, a course or a page.
  2. Dot.diagnostic-mark__dot: 10px, in the heart colour; hidden from assistive technology. While busy it is a 14px turning ring.
  3. Label.diagnostic-mark__label: “Recording”, 14px semibold; it is the button’s name.

Variants

Recording: the mark as every screen shows it.

<button class="diagnostic-mark" type="button" aria-haspopup="dialog"><span class="diagnostic-mark__dot" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="diagnostic-mark__label">Recording</span></button>
Busy (diagnostic-mark--busy, aria-busy): the dialog has closed and the browser is asking to share the tab for a screen capture.

Placement modifiers

The mark is fixed 12px from the left and 12px from the foot of the window, or the safe-area insets where they are larger. Two screens keep something at their foot, and a modifier named for the screen lifts the mark clear of it; the page that mounts the mark sets the modifier for the screen that is showing.

Sizes

One size: 44px tall (--target-comfortable), as wide as its label needs (112px for “Recording”), never narrower than 44px.

States

There is no disabled and no pressed state: the mark is on the screen exactly when the recording is on. While the dialog is open the mark is behind it and inert with the rest of the page; when the dialog closes, focus returns to the mark.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

WidthBehaviour
360pxFoot of the screen at the left, 12px in. On The Class it stands above the pinned start button. A screen that scrolls scrolls under it.
768pxIn a landscape window Class Ride stops scrolling and its foot is the bike panel, so the mark moves up onto the video’s bottom-left corner.

The mark never lies over a readout or a control at rest on the reference screens (390 × 844, 834 × 1194, 1194 × 834, 1440 × 900). It may lie over the corner of the video or the course. On a window so short that a screen scrolls, the screen’s content passes under the mark as it scrolls.

Theming

The pill is --color-panel with a --color-line-strong hairline in both themes, so it is a raised surface over a picture as over the page. The dot is --color-heart, the fill the system keeps for “live” and danger; the word beside it carries the meaning, so the colour never does alone.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button"> with aria-haspopup="dialog" (APG Dialog (Modal) pattern: the control that opens the dialog, and the element focus returns to). Its name is its label, “Recording”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the mark after the screen’s own controls (it is mounted after the screen in the document).
Enter / SpaceOpens the Diagnostic recording dialog.
EscIn the dialog: closes it; focus returns to the mark.

Focus

The shared 2px focus ring, 2px off the pill.

Labelling

The dot is aria-hidden. While a capture is taken the button carries aria-busy="true" and aria-disabled="true", its name is “Capturing…”, and pressing it does nothing; it is not disabled, so it keeps focus.

Contrast

Light theme

Label on the pill

Dark theme

Label on the pill

Motion

The busy ring turns once every --duration-loop; with reduced motion it stands still, and the word “Capturing…” still says what is happening. The dot does not pulse.

Touch

44 × 112px, above the home bar (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) and clear of a notch at the left (env(safe-area-inset-left)).

Content

  • “Recording”: one word, sentence case, never “REC”, “Diagnostics” or an icon alone.
  • “Capturing…” while the browser asks to share the tab and the picture is taken.
  • No count, no clock and no file size on the mark: those are in the dialog.
