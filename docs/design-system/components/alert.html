Alert

A message that something stands between the rider and the ride: the wrong browser, no HTTPS, Bluetooth off.

Overview

Telo Ride 1 has two alerts, both about reaching the bike. The browser notice (.browser-notice) lies at the top of The Class when the browser cannot use Web Bluetooth or the page is not served over HTTPS. The blocker (.connect-blocker) takes the pairing card’s place on Connect Bike and says why the bike cannot be reached and what to do.

A smaller piece of news that does not stop anything is an inline message. The bike lost mid-ride is the lost-bike alert.

Anatomy

  1. IconA warning triangle in --color-warn-fg; shown from 640px.
  2. TitleWhat to do, semibold: “Open Telo Ride 1 in Chrome or Edge.”
  3. BodyWhy, muted: “Bluetooth to the bike only works there — you can look around, but not ride.”
  4. ActionOne button, 36px: Copy link (raised) or Open over HTTPS (primary).

Responsive behaviour

  • Browser notice, below 640px: the icon is hidden and the action wraps under the text. From 640px: icon, text and action in one row.
  • Blocker: the width of Connect Bike’s content column at every size.

Theming

The notice is a warning-tinted panel with a hairline; the blocker is a plain panel. Icons read --color-warn-fg or --color-heart-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="alert": both appear because of something the rider just did or tried, and they block the ride. The blocker is a labelled <section>.

Keyboard

Focus

Focus is not moved; the alert is read because of its role.

Labelling

The title is the first thing read. “Copied” is announced by the button’s own text changing.
