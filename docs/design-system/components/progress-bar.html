Progress bar

How far along something is.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableCore set · no Telo Ride 1 screen uses it yet

Overview

A progress bar shows the share of a task that is done, or that something is under way.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: two blocks draw progress of their own: the segment bar in the bike panel and the bar in the roll-out card. Neither uses the generic .progress. Ibiza Flyover deliberately shows no made-up progress.

Anatomy

  1. Label and valueWhat is in progress, and how far, above the track.
  2. Track8px, full radius, raised fill.
  3. FillThe accent’s text colour (--color-volt-fg, which holds 3:1 on the track in both themes), as wide as --value.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and has no breakpoints of its own.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax; none of the three when indeterminate.

Keyboard

None.

Focus

It takes no focus.

Labelling

Named by its label.

Contrast

light theme
Fill on the track
dark theme
Fill on the track

Motion

The fill’s width eases over --duration-base; the indeterminate sweep stops with reduced motion.

Touch

Nothing is pressed.

Content

  • Name the task, not “Loading”.
  • Only show a number that is real.
