Component · The shape of a ride

Roll-out

The few seconds after the finish banner: a card at the foot of the box while the bike eases off, before Class Done opens on its own.

Overview

After the finish banner the video or the course carries on for a few seconds: the roll-out (L2-182). A card at the foot of the box says the ride is over and the summary is coming; the target resistance has eased to 4 of 32 and the stepper is off. Then Class Done opens without a press.

Use it only here. It is not a toast and not a dialog: it asks nothing of the rider.

Anatomy

  1. CardThe strong scrim, 16px radius, 12px in from the box’s left, right and foot, at most 672px wide and centred.
  2. Title“Rolling on” with the class video; “Rolling on down the promenade” in Ibiza. Semibold, in --color-on-photo.
  3. Body“Easy spin. Your summary is coming up.” at 72% of the on-photo colour; role="status".
  4. BarA 4px progress bar: how much of the roll-out has passed. role="progressbar", named “Class Done opens in a moment”.

Sizes

One size; its padding steps from 12 × 16px to 16 × 20px at 640px.

States

One state. The bar fills from 0 to 100%; the card leaves with the screen.

Rolling on

Easy spin. Your summary is coming up.

Four seconds in, with the class video: 82 W at 70 rpm, resistance 4 of 32, the stepper disabled under the box.

Responsive behaviour

  • The card keeps 12px from the box’s edges at every width and is never wider than 672px.
  • From 640px its padding grows to 16 × 20px.
  • It lies inside the box, so it never covers the bike panel.

Theming

On a picture, so the same in both themes: --color-on-photo on --color-scrim-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The body is a role="status"; the bar is a role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow.

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

Focus stays where it was. Class Done takes focus when it opens.

Labelling

The bar’s name says what it leads to: “Class Done opens in a moment”.

Contrast

light theme
Words on the strong scrim
Body at 72% on the strong scrim
dark theme
Words on the strong scrim
Body at 72% on the strong scrim

Motion

The card rises 12px and fades in over --duration-base. With reduced motion it appears without animation.

Touch

None: nothing in it is pressed. Full screen stays in the header.

Content

  • It is the “roll-out”. The title is “Rolling on”, plus where in Ibiza: “down the promenade”.
  • “Easy spin. Your summary is coming up.” Two short sentences; no countdown in numbers.
  • No second resistance readout in the card: the stepper under the box shows it.
