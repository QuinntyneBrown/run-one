Toast

A brief message that appears over the screen and goes on its own.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableCore set · no Telo Ride 1 screen uses it yet

Overview

A toast confirms something that just happened, without stopping the rider.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: no screen uses a toast. News is said in place: a status line under what it speaks of (inline message), the browser notice, or the lost-bike alert. Mid-ride nothing may pop over the bike’s numbers.

Anatomy

  1. IconThe tone, as a shape.
  2. Title and textWhat happened, then one line of detail.
  3. DismissAn icon button named “Dismiss”.

Sizes

One size.

States

A toast is shown or gone; its controls take the button’s states.

Responsive behaviour

The region is pinned to the foot of the window, 16px in, and centres its toasts; a toast is at most 384px wide.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A role="status" region that is always in the page (.toast-region); an error uses role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabTo the toast’s action and Dismiss
EscDismisses the newest toast

Focus

The standard focus ring: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px off the control.

Labelling

The title alone makes sense when read out.

Contrast

light theme

Motion

It rises 8px and fades in over --duration-base. The timer pauses on hover and focus. With reduced motion it appears at once.

Touch

Targets are at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • Past tense for what happened: “Recording saved”.
  • No toast for an error the rider must act on.
