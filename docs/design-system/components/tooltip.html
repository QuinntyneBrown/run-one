Tooltip

A short label that appears on hover and on focus.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableCore set · no Telo Ride 1 screen uses it yet

Overview

A tooltip names an icon-only control for people who can see it but do not know the icon.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: icon-only buttons (Full screen, Leave class, Cancel) carry their name in aria-label and draw no tooltip. A tablet beside a bike has no hover, so nothing may depend on one.

Anatomy

  1. AnchorThe control the tooltip describes.
  2. BubbleThe inverse surface, 12px text, 6px radius, 8px from the control.

Variants

Full screen
Above its control (the default).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="tooltip", joined to its control with aria-describedby (or aria-label on the control when the tooltip only repeats the name).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFocusing the control shows the tooltip
EscHides it until focus or the pointer moves

Focus

The standard focus ring: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px off the control.

Labelling

A tooltip never holds the only copy of something the rider must know, and never holds a control.

Contrast

light theme
Text on the bubble
dark theme
Text on the bubble

Motion

It fades in after 300ms and out at once. With reduced motion there is no fade.

Touch

Targets are at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • One to three words: the control’s name.
