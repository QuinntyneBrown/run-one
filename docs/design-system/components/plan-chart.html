Plan chart

What the bike will do: the class’s 33 segments as bars, on The Class and, with the segment being ridden, on Class Ride.

Overview

The plan of Intervals to the Beat is 33 segments: a warm-up, 17 efforts with recoveries between them, and a cool-down. The plan chart (.class-profile) draws each as a bar as wide as it lasts and as tall as its level against the highest (24).

On The Class it sits under “What the bike will do”. On Class Ride a small one sits in the segment cell (.class-segment) of the bike panel, under the name of the segment and its countdown.

Anatomy

  1. KindA 10px swatch in the segment’s colour and its name: “Warm-up”, “Effort”, “Recover”, “Cool-down”.
  2. Time leftThe display face, 36px, tabular; a role="timer" named “Recover: 0:30 left”.
  3. Segment bar6px, full radius: how much of this segment has passed, in the segment’s colour.
  4. Plan chartAn inline SVG, 20px tall here: effort bars in --color-volt, recoveries in --color-rest, warm-up and cool-down in the easy colour at half strength.

Variants

What the bike will do

~254 W average

17 efforts · 0:15 to 1:15. Resistance changes on its own; you just pedal.

On The Class: 64px tall (56px from 1024px, 64px again from 1280px), with its heading, “~254 W average” and the note.
Recover 0:30
On Class Ride: 20px tall, in the segment cell.

Sizes

Default 20px and .class-profile--lg 80px. The Class sets its own height.

States

StateSegment cell
Recover, half done
Recover 0:30
Cool-down, at the finish
Cool-down 0:00

The cell’s colour comes from one custom property, --segment, set to the kind’s colour. The chart itself has no states.

Responsive behaviour

  • The chart is as wide as its container at every width; its bars scale with it (preserveAspectRatio="none").
  • The segment cell spans the bike panel’s two columns below 768px and is its first, widest column from 768px.

Theming

Efforts read --color-volt, recoveries --color-rest, warm-up and cool-down --classes-segment-easy at 50%. All three hold 3:1 against the page and the panel in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The chart is a picture of the plan: role="img" named “Resistance profile”. The name does not carry the plan, so on The Class the note under it says it in words, and on Class Ride the segment’s name and time left do.

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

Nothing in it takes focus.

Labelling

The time left is a role="timer"; timers are not announced as they tick, so a screen reader hears it only when asked.

Motion

The segment bar’s fill moves once a second, linearly (--duration-countdown).

Touch

Nothing in it is pressed.

Content

  • The class’s hard intervals are its “efforts”: “17 efforts · 0:15 to 1:15”.
  • “What the bike will do”, then “Resistance changes on its own; you just pedal.”
  • An average is approximate: “~254 W average”.
