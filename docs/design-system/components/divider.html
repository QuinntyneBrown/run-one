Overview

A divider parts content that spacing alone does not part clearly.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: screens draw their hairlines as borders of their own blocks (under the header, over the stats, over Class Done’s facts) in --color-line; none uses .divider.

Anatomy

  1. LineA 1px rule in --color-line with 24px above and below (8px when tight).

Sizes

One size.

States

A divider has no states.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and has no breakpoints of its own.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <hr>, or role="separator" on another element. A purely decorative line is a border, not an element.

Keyboard

None.

Focus

It takes no focus.

Labelling

It needs no name.

Contrast

light theme
Line on the page (decorative)
dark theme

Motion

None.

Touch

Nothing is pressed.

Content

A divider carries no words, except the labelled variant, whose label is one or two words.
