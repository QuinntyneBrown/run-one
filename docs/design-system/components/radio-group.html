Radio group

One choice from a few, all in view: Telo Ride 1’s one choice is Ride with.

Overview

The rider chooses once per ride how to ride the class: with the Class video, or in Ibiza (Ibiza Sunset Strip). Ride with (.ride-with, L2-173) is a radio group on The Class, between the summary and the length.

Use a radio group for two to four options that each need a word of their own. It is the only radio group in the app; the native .radio input of the earlier system was removed with the forms that used it.

Anatomy

  1. Title“Ride with”, 14px medium, muted; it names the group.
  2. OptionA row at least 44px tall with a hairline border and 8px radius.
  3. MarkA 20px ring; the chosen one is filled with a 10px dot in the accent.
  4. NameSemibold.
  5. DetailOptional, 14px muted: “8.4 km · 159 m of climbing”, or why the option cannot be chosen.

Sizes

One size: options are 44px or taller.

States

StateDefaultHoverFocusChosenCannot be chosen
Option

The chosen option is edged and tinted in the accent, has aria-checked="true" and fills its mark. One that cannot be chosen is dashed and muted and says why under its name (aria-disabled="true"): “This browser can’t draw Ibiza.” Colour never carries either state alone.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px and from 1024px: the options stack.
  • 640px to 1023px: two columns.

Theming

The chosen tint is 10% of --color-volt over the page; the mark’s dot is --color-volt-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Radio Group: role="radiogroup" named by its title, holding role="radio" buttons with aria-checked and a roving tabindex.

Focus

The ring is drawn on the whole option.

Labelling

An option that cannot be chosen stays focusable and readable (aria-disabled, not disabled), so its reason is heard.

Motion

The mark’s dot scales in over --duration-fast.

Touch

Each option is at least 44px tall and the full width of its column.

Content

  • The group’s title is “Ride with”. The options are “Class video” and “Ibiza Sunset Strip”.
  • A detail is facts (“8.4 km · 159 m of climbing”) or the reason it is unavailable.
