Component · The shape of a ride

Finish banner

The banner at 20:00: “Finish!” and the ride’s output on a band with a chequered edge, across the video’s or the course’s box.

Overview

When the clock reaches 20:00 the finish banner lies across the box for 3.2 seconds (L2-181): the word “Finish!”, the ride’s output in kJ and its time. Meanwhile the target resistance eases off and the stepper is disabled; the roll-out follows, then Class Done opens on its own.

It is the one celebratory moment of the ride and it is for one rider: no place, position or podium. Do not use it for anything but the end of the class.

Anatomy

  1. BandThe inverse surface (--color-bg-inverse) with ink text, 120% of the box’s width, turned −3°, so it runs off both edges.
  2. Chequered edge20px squares of ink, above and below the band.
  3. Title“Finish!” in the display face, upper case: 88px from 640px and 120px from 1024px.
  4. OutputThe numerals in --finish-banner-accent, 36px (60px from 640px), then “kJ output”.
  5. Time“· 20:00” at 65% of the text colour.
  6. StatusThe band is aria-hidden; a visually hidden role="status" says “Finished. 351 kJ.”

Sizes

One size; the type steps with the window (see Responsive).

States

The banner has one state. It is either across the box or not there. While it shows, Pause and Leave are gone from the header and the resistance stepper is disabled.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px: 8px padding above and below the words; output numerals 36px, the result line 20px.
  • From 640px: 16px padding; “Finish!” 88px, output 60px, result line 24px. From 1024px: “Finish!” 120px.
  • The result line never wraps (white-space: nowrap); the band is wider than the box, so it has the room.

Theming

The band is the inverse of the page in both themes: bone in the dark theme, ink in the light. The output’s accent follows it: --palette-signal-deep on the light band of the dark theme, --palette-signal on the dark band of the light theme, for 3:1 at the least on its numerals (L2-181 AC9). The words are --color-ink on the band.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The band is decoration (aria-hidden="true"). The result is spoken by one role="status": “Finished. 351 kJ.”

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

Focus stays where it was. The banner takes no pointer input and holds no control.

Labelling

The status carries the output with its unit. Class Done repeats it as its heading’s neighbour, so nothing is lost if the announcement is missed.

Contrast

light theme
Words on the band (4.5:1)
Output numerals on the band (large, 3:1)
dark theme
Words on the band (4.5:1)
Output numerals on the band (large, 3:1)

Motion

The band slides in from the left over --duration-base with --ease-enter. With reduced motion it fades in instead (finish-banner-fade).

Touch

None: nothing in it is pressed.

Content

  • “Finish!”, one word, with the exclamation mark.
  • “351 kJ output · 20:00”: the number, “kJ output”, a middle dot, the time. It is the ride’s “output”, never “score”, “energy” or “work”.
  • No place, rank, medal or comparison with other riders. A new best is Class Done’s to say, in gold.
