Button

Buttons do things: pair the bike, start the class, pause, leave, finish. One primary button per screen.

Overview

A button (.btn) triggers an action. Each screen has exactly one primary button, the one thing to do next: “Pair Echelon EX-5s”, “Start class”, “Done”. Everything else is raised (a secondary action on the page or over a picture), quiet (a way out, in the diagnostic dialog) or an icon button in the header.

Links that only go somewhere are drawn as buttons when they are the screen’s next step (the app has no inline links). The 48px step buttons of the resistance stepper are their own block.

Anatomy

  1. Container40px tall by default, 8px radius, 16px side padding. A button shorter than 44px still takes touches across 44px.
  2. Icon (optional)16px, before the label, in the label’s colour.
  3. Label14px semibold: a verb, or a verb and its object.

Variants

Primary (signal orange, ink text), raised, quiet, and the plain button.
HTML
<div class="ds-row"><button class="btn btn--primary" type="button">Start class</button><button class="btn btn--raised" type="button">Try again</button><button class="btn btn--quiet" type="button">Close</button><button class="btn" type="button">Reload</button></div>
Icon buttons (.icon-btn, 36px) and Leave, which turns to the danger colour on hover. Each has an aria-label.

A toggle: aria-pressed carries the state (Music, on the course).

Sizes

Small 36px (header), default 40px, large 48px (page actions), extra large 56px (the one start action).

Responsive behaviour

  • The start action is full width in its column at every size, pinned to the foot of the window below 1024px on The Class.
  • On Class Ride, below 640px, Pause and Leave are 48px squares and Pause drops its label; from 640px they draw 36px tall with 44px of touch area.
  • Done is full width below 640px and 288px wide from there.

Theming

Variants change component tokens (--btn-bg, --btn-fg, --btn-bg-hover), never properties. Primary is --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button">, or an <a> with the same classes when it goes to another screen.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the button
EnterActivates a button or a link
SpaceActivates a button

Focus

A 2px ring in --color-focus-ring, 2px off the button, on :focus-visible.

Labelling

Icon-only buttons have an aria-label. A toggle keeps one name and changes aria-pressed. A busy button sets aria-busy.

Motion

A press scales the button to 98%; hover fills fade over --duration-fast.

Touch

At least 44 × 44px of touch area on every button; 48px for anything pressed mid-ride.

Content

  • A verb, or a verb and its object, in sentence case: “Start class”, “Pair Echelon EX-5s”, “Skip flyover”, “Try again”.
  • “Done” ends a ride’s result; “Leave class” leaves a ride.
  • No “OK”, “Submit” or “Click here”.
