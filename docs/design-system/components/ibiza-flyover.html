Component · The shape of a ride

Ibiza Flyover

The screen before a ride in Ibiza: the course seen from above, start to finish, while it loads. A bar above, a band below, and no word on the course.

Overview

Ibiza Flyover is shown only before a ride in Ibiza (L2-179). The camera flies Ibiza Sunset Strip from the grid to the finish line in 9 seconds and lands on the grid; the course loads meanwhile, so the ride has no wait of its own. The rider can Skip flyover or Cancel at any moment.

It never holds the ride: it gives way if the course cannot be drawn, and it is not shown at all with reduced motion. It borrows a race's shape and nothing else: one rider, no other riders, positions, laps or places (ADR 0049).

Do not use it before a ride with the class video, and do not use its bar or band on another screen: App header is the header everywhere else.

Anatomy

Ibiza Flyover two seconds into the flightpages/ibiza-flyover/default
  1. BarA row of its own, 56px tall and 64px from 640px: the lockup, Skip flyover and Cancel.
  2. Skip flyoverA raised small button, 44px tall, pushed to the right. Goes straight to the grid.
  3. CancelA 44px icon button named “Cancel”. Goes back to The Class.
  4. SceneThe course from above, edge to edge between the bar and the band. Only the loading line may lie on it.
  5. BandA row of its own, at least 160px tall on a phone and 144px from 640px.
  6. Title and captionThe course’s name in the display face, and a caption that names the place the camera is over. The caption is a polite live region.
  7. StatusWhat is true of the bike: “Echelon EX-5s connected”, then “Bike ready · lining up” in the accent.

Variants

There is one flyover, of the one course. It has no variants.

Sizes

The flyover takes the whole window; its type steps with the window (see Responsive).

Responsive behaviour

  • Every width: three rows: bar, scene, band. The scene takes the height the other two leave; the page never scrolls.
  • Below 640px: the band stacks the title and caption over the status, and keeps clear of the home bar (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)). The title is 36px.
  • From 640px: the band is one row, the status on the right; the title is 48px, and 60px from 1024px.

Theming

The bar and the band stand on the page background (--color-ink) in both themes; the scene is a picture and does not change. The ready status reads --color-volt-fg, the lost-bike line --color-heart-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page: <header> (bar), <main> (scene) and <footer> (band). The course’s name is the page’s <h1>; the picture has role="img" and the name “Ibiza Sunset Strip from above”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkip flyover, then Cancel, then Try again when the bike is lost
EnterActivates the focused control
EscCancels: back to The Class

Focus

Focus starts on Skip flyover. The ring is the standard 2px ring, drawn on the bar, never on the picture.

Labelling

The caption is aria-live="polite", so each place is read once as it changes. The loading line is role="status"; the lost bike is role="alert".

Motion

The flight is the screen. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the screen is not shown: The Class goes straight to the countdown (L2-179 AC9).

Touch

Skip flyover and Cancel are 44px tall; Try again is 48px.

Content

  • The status says only what is true: “Echelon EX-5s connected”, then “Bike ready · lining up”. No made-up progress (“setting warm-up resistance”, “nearly there”).
  • A caption is a place and one short phrase, joined by “ · ”: “The Strip · every effort climbs past the clubs”.
  • The button is “Skip flyover”, never “Skip” alone. The screen is the “flyover”; the course is “Ibiza Sunset Strip”.
