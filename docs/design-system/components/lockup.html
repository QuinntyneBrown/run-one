Lockup

The mark and the wordmark together: TELO RIDE 1, with “RIDE 1” in signal orange, on every screen.

Overview

The lockup (.lockup) is the product’s name as it is drawn: a ring with a crank (the mark) and the wordmark TELO RIDE 1 in the italic display face, “RIDE 1” in the accent. It opens every screen’s header. It is “1”, never “One” or “I”.

It is an image, not a link: there is no home to go to that the screen’s own controls do not already reach.

Anatomy

  1. MarkA 48-unit SVG: a ring in the text colour, a crank and hub in --color-volt. 26px by default.
  2. TELOBarlow Condensed, black italic, upper case, 24px, in the text colour.
  3. Ride 1The same face in --color-volt-fg, 0.18em after “TELO”.

By context: 24px wordmark in most headers, 20px on Class Ride, 24px in the flyover’s bar.

States

The lockup is not interactive.

Responsive behaviour

  • On Class Ride the wordmark hides below 384px and the mark stands alone; the accessible name stays “Telo Ride 1”.
  • Elsewhere it is the same at every width.

Theming

The ring and “TELO” follow the text colour; the crank is the accent in both themes; “Ride 1” is --color-volt-fg, the deeper signal on paper.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="img" with aria-label="Telo Ride 1"; the SVG inside is hidden.

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

It takes no focus.

Labelling

One name for the whole lockup, so a screen reader does not read “TELO” and “Ride 1” apart.

Content

  • Written out it is “Telo Ride 1”: a capital T and R, a space, the digit.
  • Never “Telo Ride One”, “Telo Ride I”, “TeloRide” or “Telo Ride” alone.
