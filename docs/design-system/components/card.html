Overview

A card sets one subject apart: a title, a line or two, perhaps a picture and an action.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: there is one class, so there is no grid of cards. The surfaces the screens do use have their own blocks: the bike card on Connect Bike (Device list), the cells of the bike panel and the roll-out card.

Anatomy

  1. Surface--color-panel, 16px radius.
  2. Eyebrow12px, muted: what kind of thing this is.
  3. Title16px semibold.
  4. Meta14px, muted.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <article> or a list item. A clickable card holds one real link in its title, stretched over the card (.card__link); the card itself is not a control.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabTo the card’s link
EnterFollows it

Focus

The standard focus ring: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px off the control.

Labelling

The title is a heading at the level its place in the page calls for.

Contrast

light theme
Text on a panel
Muted text on a panel
dark theme
Text on a panel
Muted text on a panel

Motion

State changes fade over --duration-fast; reduced motion makes them instant.

Touch

Targets are at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • One subject per card; the title names it.
