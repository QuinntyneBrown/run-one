Component · Diagnostic recording

Diagnostic panel

The one dialog of the diagnostic recording: add a note of what the bike’s own display reads, capture the screen, and save the files. It is the platform’s <dialog> element holding one block, diagnostic-panel.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDiagnostic aid · off for every rider

Overview

The recording mark opens this dialog over whatever screen is showing; the ride carries on behind it (L2-178 AC11). It is for the person diagnosing a bike, not for the rider, and exists only with the app opened at /?diagnose.

  • The dialog is <dialog class="diagnostic-dialog">, opened with showModal(). The browser gives the top layer, the inert page behind, the focus trap, Escape and the dialog role. It is not the .scrim + .dialog shell Telo Ride had, which this system removed, and not the CDK’s dialog; ADR 0048 gives the reason, measured in the initial bundle.
  • The panel is .diagnostic-panel, a presentation block: it takes the count, the status and whether a capture is possible, and emits add note, capture, save and close.
  • There is no list of entries, no viewer and no replay on screen: the recording is read in the file the person saves.

Anatomy

  1. Title__title: “Diagnostic recording”, 18px semibold; it names the dialog (aria-labelledby).
  2. Count__count: the number of entries, with tabular figures; at the limit it says how many were dropped.
  3. Note field__field holding __label, __input (44px, 200 characters at most) and __hint.
  4. Actions__actions: Add note and Capture screen, raised buttons 48px tall. A __hint under them says why Capture screen is off, when it is.
  5. Status__status: one polite live region, a line tall when empty; __status--warn with __status-icon for a failure.
  6. Privacy__privacy: what a saved recording holds, above a hairline, before anything is saved.
  7. Footer__footer: Save files (primary) and Close (quiet), at the end of the row.

Variants

One form. What changes is its state: the field, the status line, whether Capture screen is possible and what the count says.

Sizes

The dialog is a sheet as wide as the window at its foot below 640px, and a centred card --layout-text-md (448px) wide from 640px, never wider than the window less 32px. It is as tall as the panel needs (about 460px; 524px at 320px wide with the capture line) and never taller than the window less 24px (32px from 640px); past that it scrolls inside. Buttons are btn--lg (48px) and the field is 44px tall with 16px text.

Responsive behaviour

WidthBehaviour
320pxA sheet at the foot of the window, full width, with the top corners rounded; the two actions sit side by side and the privacy text takes four lines. It fits a 320 × 568 window without scrolling.
640pxA centred card 448px wide with all four corners rounded.
1194 × 834The same card, centred over the screen; the page behind does not scroll or move.

Theming

The card is --color-panel with a --color-line hairline and --shadow-4; the backdrop is --color-scrim on the dialog’s ::backdrop. The field is a well in --color-ink. Secondary text (count, hints, privacy) is --color-muted, never the fainter tone, so it passes 4.5:1 in both themes. A warning status is --color-warn-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The platform’s modal <dialog> opened with showModal() (APG Dialog (Modal) pattern), named by its title through aria-labelledby. The page behind is inert without any attribute of the app’s.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabField → Add note → Capture screen → Save files → Close, and round again; focus cannot leave the dialog.
EnterIn the field: adds the note, as Add note does, when the field is not empty.
EscCloses the dialog; focus returns to the recording mark.

Focus

On opening, focus is in the note field. After Add note it goes back to the field. After a capture the dialog opens again by itself. Closing returns focus to the mark.

Labelling

The field has a visible <label> and its hint through aria-describedby; it has no placeholder. The status is one role="status" region that is in the dialog from the start and whose text is replaced, so each message is said once. A failure’s icon is aria-hidden; its sentence says what failed. When the browser cannot capture the screen, Capture screen is disabled and described by the line under the actions, which is not the status.

Motion

None of its own: the dialog appears and goes without animation, so a screen capture never catches it half open.

Touch

Buttons are 48px tall and the field 44px; the sheet keeps its footer clear of the home bar (env(safe-area-inset-bottom)).

Content

The quoted strings are L2-178’s, word for word.

  • Title: “Diagnostic recording”.
  • Count: “1,284 entries since 18:00”; at the limit “20,000 entries · the oldest 4,312 were dropped”. Thousands take a comma.
  • Field label: “What the bike’s display reads”. Hint: “For example: bike shows 82 rpm, resistance 14”. No placeholder.
  • Status: “Note added” · “Screen capture 1 kept” · “Saved 3 files: the recording and 2 screen captures.”
  • Warnings: “The browser didn’t share the screen, so no capture was kept. The ride carries on.” · “The files couldn’t be saved. Try again.”
  • No capture possible: “This browser can’t capture the screen. Notes and the recording still work.”
  • Privacy: “The recording holds your bike’s name and identifier and every Bluetooth message. A screen capture holds whatever is on the screen. Nothing is sent anywhere: the files are saved only when you press Save files.”
  • Buttons: Add note, Capture screen, Save files, Close.
  • A note, a device name and an error are data: they are rendered as text, never as HTML.
