Inline message

One line of news, with an icon, right under the thing it is about. The ride goes on.

Overview

An inline message (.inline-message) tells the rider that something is short without stopping anything: the video cannot play, the course cannot be drawn, the class cannot set this bike’s resistance, the ride could not be saved. It stands 8px under what it speaks of.

If the rider cannot go on, use an alert. If the bike is lost mid-ride, the lost-bike alert covers the screen.

Anatomy

  1. Icon16px, in the warning colour.
  2. Text14px, muted: what is lost, then what still works.

Variants

The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.

Warning (.inline-message--warn): the only tone the screens use.
HTML
<p class="inline-message inline-message--warn" role="status"><svg aria-hidden="true" class="icon icon--md inline-message__icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24">…</svg><span class="inline-message__text">The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.</span></p>

Sizes

One size: 14px text, 16px icon.

States

WhereMessage
Under the video

The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.

Under the course

Ibiza can’t be drawn. The bike will still follow the class.

Under the bike panel

The class can’t set this bike’s resistance. Set it by hand.

Under Class Done’s facts

This ride could not be saved on this device. Your result is above.

In place of The Class

The class can’t be loaded. Reload Telo Ride 1 to try again.

Responsive behaviour

The text wraps; the icon stays on the first line.

Theming

Text --color-muted, icon --color-warn-fg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status" when the ride goes on; role="alert" when the screen cannot do its job (the class that cannot be loaded).

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

It takes no focus.

Labelling

The icon is hidden; the sentence stands alone.

Content

  • Two short sentences: what is lost, what still works. “The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.”
  • Plain words; no codes, no “Error”.
