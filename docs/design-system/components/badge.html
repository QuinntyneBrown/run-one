Badge

A short label for a status or a count.

Overview

A badge (.pill) tags something with a word or a number.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: statuses are plain words in a colour that passes on its own: “Full control” and “Beta” on Connect Bike, “On target” in the bike panel, “New best output” on Class Done. None is drawn as a pill.

Anatomy

  1. PillFull radius, 2 × 8px padding, 12px medium.
  2. DotOptional: repeats the tone as a shape beside the word.
  3. LabelOne or two words.

Variants

SoloOn targetConnectedBetaNo bikeNew best
Neutral, accent, ok, warning, danger, gold. Gold is for earned things only.

Sizes

One size.

States

A badge is not interactive. One that can be pressed is a chip.

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and has no breakpoints of its own.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Connected

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Text. A count has an aria-label that says what is counted.

Keyboard

None.

Focus

It takes no focus.

Labelling

The word carries the meaning; the colour repeats it.

  • A state, not an action.
