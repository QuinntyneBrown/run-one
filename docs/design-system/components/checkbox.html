Checkbox

A yes or no that waits for a submit, alone or in a group.

Overview

A checkbox records a choice that takes effect later, with the form. A setting that applies at once is a switch.

Where Telo Ride 1 stands: no screen has a checkbox. The diagnostic panel’s actions are buttons.

Anatomy

  1. Box20px, 4px radius; the accent when checked, with an ink tick.
  2. LabelBeside the box; pressing it toggles the box.
  3. HintOptional, 12px, under the label.

Variants

Single.

Sizes

One size.

States

StateOffOnFocusInvalidDisabled
Checkbox

Responsive behaviour

It takes the width of its container and has no breakpoints of its own.

Theming

It reads semantic tokens only, so it follows the theme with no rule of its own.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <input type="checkbox"> inside its <label>; a group is a <fieldset> with a <legend>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
SpaceToggles
TabMoves to the next control

Focus

The standard focus ring: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px off the control.

Labelling

The label is the name; a hint is joined with aria-describedby.

Contrast

light theme
Tick on the accent
Box edge on the page
dark theme
Tick on the accent
Box edge on the page

Motion

State changes fade over --duration-fast; reduced motion makes them instant.

Touch

Targets are at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • Word the label so that checked means yes.
  • No negatives: “Attach…”, not “Don’t attach…”.
