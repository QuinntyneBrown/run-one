Bike panel

The bike’s numbers under the video or the course: the segment, power, cadence and the resistance stepper.

Overview

The bike panel (.class-bike) is the foot of Class Ride. It holds four cells: the segment being ridden, Power, Cadence and the resistance stepper. Power and cadence are what the bike reports, once a second; the hint under each says how the rider is doing against the plan.

Use a cell (.class-stat) only for a number the bike reports during a ride. A number that describes a finished ride is a stat.

Anatomy

  1. PanelA grid with 12px gaps: two columns below 768px (segment and stepper span both), four from 768px (1.2fr, 1fr, 1fr, auto).
  2. Cell--color-panel, 12px radius, 12 × 16px padding.
  3. Label12px, muted: “Power”, “Cadence”.
  4. Number and unitThe display face with tabular numerals, 48px; the unit 14px and faint, on the number’s baseline.
  5. Hint12px under the number: “On target”, “Aim 90”, or how far off (“-39 W”).

Variants

One panel. The cells are the same block; the segment cell and the stepper are documented on their own pages.

Sizes

One size: a rider reads it at arm’s length.

On target is within 10% of the plan’s watts: medium weight in --color-volt-fg. Under target is the difference in the warning colour, with its sign (.class-stat__hint--under); over target is the difference in medium weight (--over). At the finish and in the roll-out the hints are gone: there is no target left.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: two columns; the segment and the stepper each take a full row, Power and Cadence share one.
  • From 768px: one row of four. In a landscape window the panel keeps its height and the video’s box takes the rest.

Theming

Cells are --color-panel on the page. On-target reads --color-volt-fg, the deeper signal in the light theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text in reading order: label, number, unit, hint. The panel is not a live region: numbers that change every second would never stop talking.

Keyboard

It takes no keyboard input: nothing in it can be focused.

Focus

Only the stepper’s buttons take focus.

Labelling

The hint puts the state in words (“On target”, “-39 W”), so the colour is never alone.

Motion

Numbers change in place, without animation. Tabular numerals keep them from shifting.

Touch

See the stepper: its buttons are 48px.

Content

  • “Power” in W, “Cadence” in rpm. The bike’s level is “resistance”; the plan’s is “target resistance”.
  • “On target”, “Aim 90”, “-39 W”: a hint is two words or a signed difference.
  • Whole numbers only; no decimals mid-ride.
