Resistance stepper

Lower, the level, raise: the bike’s resistance, and the rider’s way to nudge it mid-ride.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableIn the mocks

Overview

The class’s plan sets the target resistance for every segment; the rider just pedals. The stepper (.class-resist) shows the bike’s resistance, 1 to 32, and lets the rider step it down or up by one when a segment is too much or too little.

It is the only stepper in Telo Ride 1. It is off (both buttons disabled) at the finish and through the roll-out, and when the bike gives no control of its resistance.

Anatomy

  1. Cell--color-panel, 12px radius, 12px padding; the last cell of the bike panel.
  2. Lower / Raise48 × 48px buttons on --color-raised, 8px radius, with a 20px minus or plus.
  3. LevelThe display face, 36px, tabular.
  4. “of 32”12px, muted: the scale’s top, so “13” is never a bare number.

Variants

One stepper. It has no variants.

Sizes

One size: 48px buttons, the size for anything pressed mid-ride.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px: a full row of the bike panel, the buttons at its two ends.
  • From 768px: the panel’s last column, as wide as its content.
  • The buttons are 48px at every width.

Theming

The cell is --color-panel, the buttons --color-raised, hovering to --color-line. Disabled buttons are 30% opaque.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Two <button>s around a value. Not a spinbutton: there is nothing to type, and the bike, not the page, owns the value.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabLower, then Raise
Space EnterSteps one level

Focus

The standard 2px ring on each button.

Labelling

The buttons are named “Lower resistance” and “Raise resistance”; the value’s aria-label is “Resistance 13 of 32”, or “Resistance unknown”.

Motion

A press scales the button to 96%. The level changes when the bike reports it, not when the button is pressed.

Touch

48 × 48px (--target-ride), 8px apart at the least.

Content

  • The word is “resistance”; what the plan asks for is “target resistance”. Never “gear”, “level” alone or “difficulty”.
  • “13 of 32”; an unknown level is an em dash.
  • When the class cannot set it: “The class can’t set this bike’s resistance. Set it by hand.”
