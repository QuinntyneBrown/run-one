Class Done

The ride’s result: the output in kJ, in gold for a new best, the facts, the 17 efforts and Done.

Overview

Class Done (.class-done) opens on its own after the roll-out. Its headline is the ride’s output in kJ. The verdict above it says how it stands against the rider’s best; a new best is gold, the one colour kept for earned things. Under it: time, average watts, how much of the ride was on target, and the class’s 17 efforts as bars. Done returns to The Class.

Anatomy

Class Done with a new bestpages/class-done/default
  1. VerdictThe page’s <h1>, 16px: “New best output” (semibold, gold), “Your best is still 342 kJ”, or “Class done. That’s your number to beat.”
  2. OutputThe display face at 88px (120px from 640px) and “kJ” at 36px, muted. Gold only for a new best.
  3. FactsA <dl> over a hairline: Time, Average, On target, Previous best. Labels 12px muted, values 24px display.
  4. EffortsAn ordered list drawn as bars: each bar’s height is its watts against the strongest; the strongest is signal orange and says “your strongest” in text.
  5. DonePrimary, 56px; full width on a phone, 288px from 640px.

Responsive behaviour

  • One column, at most the narrow container’s width, at every size; 32px above and below, 56px from 1024px.
  • At 320px seventeen figures do not fit side by side: each is set upright along its bar, reading upwards (L2-169 AC6).
  • Done is full width below 640px and 288px wide from 640px.

Theming

Gold is --color-gold-fg, deeper in the light theme. Bars are --color-raised-toned with the strongest in --color-volt.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page: the verdict is the <h1>, “Your 17 efforts, average watts” an <h2>. The chart is a real <ol>: each item reads “Effort 9: 427 W, your strongest”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFull screen, then Done
EnterDone: back to The Class

Focus

The standard ring on Full screen and Done. The verdict is the first thing in the page, so it is the first thing read.

Labelling

The strongest effort says so in text, not only in orange. The not-saved notice is role="status".

Motion

The bars rise and fade in, staggered. With reduced motion they are simply there.

Touch

Done is 56px tall.

Content

  • The result is the ride’s “output”, in kJ. Never “score”, “points” or “calories”.
  • Verdicts: “New best output” · “Your best is still 342 kJ” · “Class done. That’s your number to beat.”
  • “This ride could not be saved on this device. Your result is above.”
  • The button is “Done”.
