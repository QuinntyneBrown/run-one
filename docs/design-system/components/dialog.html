Dialog

What is laid over a screen and must be answered: the lost-bike alert, and the diagnostic recording’s dialog.

Overview

The rider’s app has one overlay: Lost the bike (.class-lost), which covers Class Ride when the bike stops answering, and offers Reconnect. The ride and the video (or the course) are paused under it.

The only true dialog is no part of the rider’s app: the Diagnostic recording dialog, the platform’s <dialog> element, open only with ?diagnose (ADR 0048). Buttons never open inline forms or hand-rolled modals.

Sizes

The card is at most 384px wide.

States

It is shown or not. Reconnecting takes the rider back to the ride where it stopped.

Responsive behaviour

The scrim covers the screen under the header at every size; the card is centred with 24px at its sides.

Theming

The scrim is ink in the dark theme and paper in the light, at 85%, so the card’s text keeps the page’s contrast.
