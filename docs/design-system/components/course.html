Component · The shape of a ride

Course

Ibiza Sunset Strip in the video’s box: the course drawn as the rider rides, with one Music button in its corner.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableIn the mocks

Overview

In a ride in Ibiza the course takes the place of the class video: the same box, by the same rules, with no video (L2-174). It is the only course. One track plays in the background, Beautiful Boom Bap, and the Music button in the box’s top-right corner turns it off and on (L2-176).

The countdown, the finish banner and the roll-out are drawn inside this box.

Anatomy

  1. Box16:9, 12px radius, on --color-raised. In a landscape window from 768px it takes the height the header and the bike panel leave instead.
  2. SceneThe canvas the renderer draws into, edge to edge (.class-course__scene). In the mocks a still of the solo ride stands in for it.
  3. MusicA raised small button, at least 44 × 44px, 12px in from the top-right corner. aria-pressed says whether the track plays.

Variants

One course, one box. It has no variants.

Sizes

The box takes its size from Class Ride’s layout.

States

Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip

Loading: the course’s still under “Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip” (role="status"), two seconds into the ride. The bike already follows the class.

Paused

Paused: a 70% ink tint and “Paused”. The lost-bike alert reads “The ride is paused.” in Ibiza.

Ibiza can’t be drawn. The bike will still follow the class.

The course cannot be drawn: the still stays in the box and this notice stands 8px under it. The ride goes on.
OnOffUnavailable
Music

Off is not pressed and strikes the note through. Unavailable is disabled, with no alert: a ride without music is still the ride (L2-176 AC7).

Responsive behaviour

  • Portrait, and landscape below 768px: the box is 16:9 at the column’s width; the page scrolls.
  • Landscape from 768px: nothing scrolls; the box takes the height left, never under 128px.
  • The Music button keeps its corner and its 44px at every width.

Theming

The scene is a picture and does not change with the theme. The loading line is --color-on-photo on --color-scrim-photo in both themes; the paused tint is 70% of the page colour under the page’s text colour; the Music button is the raised button of the current theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The scene has role="img" and the name “Ibiza Sunset Strip”. Music is a toggle button (aria-pressed).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches Music after the header’s controls
Space EnterTurns the music off or on

Focus

The standard ring on the Music button; it is drawn over the picture and keeps 3:1 against it through the ring’s offset.

Labelling

The button’s name is “Music” in both states; aria-pressed carries the state, and the struck-through note repeats it for the eye. The loading line and the notice are role="status".

Contrast

light theme
“Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip” on the photo scrim
Music label on the raised button
dark theme
“Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip” on the photo scrim
Music label on the raised button

Motion

The course itself moves with the rider. The box adds no motion of its own.

Touch

Music is at least 44 × 44px.

Content

  • “Music”, a noun, in both states. Not “Mute”, “Sound on” or the track’s name.
  • “Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip”, no ellipsis. “Ibiza can’t be drawn. The bike will still follow the class.”
  • The course is “Ibiza Sunset Strip”; a ride on it is “in Ibiza”.
