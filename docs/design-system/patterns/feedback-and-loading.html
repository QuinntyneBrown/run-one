Pattern

Feedback and loading

Say it where it happened, in words, and never invent progress.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • When to use what
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive
  • Accessibility

Overview

Feedback in Telo Ride 1 is small and local. A button that is working says so on itself; a wait whose subject is a picture says so over the picture; news stands under the thing it is about. There are no toasts and no skeletons on any screen.

When to use what

SituationUseExample
A button started something that takes a momentThe busy button: a spinner and a changed label, aria-busy.“Pairing…”
The browser is doing something of its ownA status in the card.“Looking for bikes nearby…” while the browser’s list is open
A picture is loadingA status line over its still.“Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip”
Something is short but the ride goes onAn inline message under its subject.“The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.”
The rider cannot go onAn alert, or the lost-bike alert mid-ride.“Bluetooth is off”
Something was doneThe control says so itself.Copy link → “Copied” with a tick
The ride endedThe finish banner, then Class Done.“Finish!”

Structure

Echelon EX-5s

EX5S-0bf669f1

Pairing: the card names the bike; the button is busy.

Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip

A picture loading, said in words over its still.

Open Telo Ride 1 in Chrome or Edge. Bluetooth to the bike only works there — you can look around, but not ride.

Done: the button itself reads “Copied”.

States

  • Loading: words, with a spinner only inside the control or card that is waiting.
  • Success: the next screen, or the control’s own label. Pairing lands on The Class; a ride lands on Class Done.
  • Failure: what to do, and one action: Try again, Reconnect, Reload.

Responsive behaviour

Feedback keeps its place at every size: a notice stays under its subject, a busy button keeps its width.

Accessibility

  • role="status" for news the ride survives; role="alert" for what blocks it.
  • A busy control has aria-busy="true" and stays focusable.
  • Power, cadence and the clock are not live regions.
  • A spinner stands still with reduced motion; its words carry the state.

Content

  • A present participle and an ellipsis while working: “Pairing…”. “Loading” and the thing, without one, over a picture.
  • Past tense when done: “Copied”.
  • No percentages unless they are real.
