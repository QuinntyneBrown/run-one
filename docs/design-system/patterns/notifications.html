Pattern

Notifications

The app sends nothing and pops nothing up. What the rider needs to know is on the screen they are looking at.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Telo Ride 1 has no backend, no account and no other riders, so there is nothing to notify about: no push messages, no notification centre, no toasts. Everything that would be a notification elsewhere is a state of a screen here.

When to use what

The newsWhere it shows
The browser cannot reach the bikeThe browser notice at the top of The Class, until it is fixed.
The bike is connected, or notThe bike link in The Class’s header.
The bike was lostThe lost-bike alert.
Something is shortAn inline message under its subject.
A new bestClass Done’s verdict and output, in gold.
The recording is onThe recording mark, with ?diagnose only.

Structure

Open Telo Ride 1 in Chrome or Edge. Bluetooth to the bike only works there — you can look around, but not ride.

The one persistent notice: it stays while the cause stays, and cannot be dismissed.
TELORide 1 No bike · Connect
The bike’s state lives in the header.

States

A notice is there while its cause is there and gone when it is not. None is dismissed by hand, and none times out.

Responsive behaviour

The browser notice wraps its action under its text on a phone.

Accessibility

  • A notice that appears is announced once by its role; it is not repeated.
  • Nothing appears over the control the rider is using.

Content

  • State the fact and the fix; no “Heads up!”, no badges, no counts.
