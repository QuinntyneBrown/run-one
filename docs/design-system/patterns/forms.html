Pattern

Forms

The rider’s app asks one question, Ride with, and has no forms. The diagnostic aid has one optional line of text.

Overview

Riding needs no typing. There is no sign-in, no profile, no settings and no custom workout, so the only input in the rider’s app is the Ride with choice on The Class, which applies at once. The diagnostic recording’s dialog has one text input, the note. The core set’s form field, text field and form layout are ready for the day a screen needs them.

When to use what

NeedUse
Choose between two or three things, all in viewRadio group: Ride with.
Turn one thing on or off over a pictureA toggle button with aria-pressed: Music.
Nudge a number the bike ownsThe resistance stepper.
One optional line of textA labelled input and its own button: the diagnostic note.
Anything longerA requirement, a mock, then a CDK Dialog or a screen of its own. No inline forms.

Structure

Ride with

Ride with: a choice that needs no Save.
The diagnostic note, ready to adddialogs/diagnostic-recording/note-ready

States

  • Ride with: chosen, not chosen, cannot be chosen (with the reason).
  • The note: empty (Add note disabled), ready, added.

Responsive behaviour

Ride with stacks on a phone and from 1024px, and is two columns between. The diagnostic dialog is a sheet on a phone.

Accessibility

  • Every control has a visible label; a group has a name (aria-labelledby).
  • A choice that cannot be made stays focusable and says why.
  • Changes that apply at once need no confirmation and are announced by the control’s own state.

Content

  • Labels are nouns: “Ride with”, “Music”. Buttons name the outcome: “Add note”.
  • Do not ask for what the bike or the browser already knows.
