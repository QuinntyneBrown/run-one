Content and tone

How the foundations’ words are put to work on each screen: one voice, from “Connect your bike” to “Done”.

Overview

Content holds the vocabulary and the mechanics. This page shows the voice across a ride: direct before it, quiet during it, plain after it.

When to use what

MomentToneExample
Before the rideDirect, practical.“Pedal once to wake it up.”
StartingBrief; the bike does the work.“The plan sets your resistance. Just pedal.”
DuringNumbers and two-word hints; no sentences.“On target” · “Aim 90” · “-39 W”
Something is shortCalm; what still works.“The bike will still follow the class.”
The finishThe one loud word.“Finish!”
AfterThe rider’s own number, plainly.“Class done. That’s your number to beat.”

States

The words for each state are in the microcopy library.

Responsive behaviour

Copy does not change with the window. Where room runs out a word is hidden from sight but still read (“· Change”, “/ 20:00”, Pause’s label).

Accessibility

  • Plain words at a low reading level; no idiom a translation would lose.
  • A hidden label is hidden from sight only, never from a screen reader.
  • Units are written as the rider says them: “kJ”, “W”, “rpm”.

Content

  • The same word for the same thing: output, efforts, resistance, target resistance, flyover, countdown, finish banner, roll-out.
  • The product is “Telo Ride 1”.
