The shape of a ride

A ride runs like a race against nobody: flyover, countdown, the class, finish banner, roll-out, Class Done. One rider, no positions, laps or places.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

A ride borrows a race’s shape and nothing else (ADR 0049, L2-179 to L2-182). The shape gives the 20 minutes a start and an end the rider can feel: the course seen from above, three lights, a banner at the line, a few seconds to spin down. It is always solo, so the rider can pause, leave or finish at any time.

The pieces: Ibiza Flyover, Countdown, the class video or the course over the bike panel, the finish banner, the roll-out and Class Done.

When to use what

The rider choseThe ride is
Class videoCountdown → the class with its video → finish banner → roll-out → Class Done. No flyover.
Ibiza Sunset StripIbiza Flyover → countdown → the class on the course, with music → finish banner → roll-out → Class Done.
Ibiza, with reduced motionAs Ibiza, without the flyover.
Ibiza, and the course cannot be drawnThe flyover gives way; the ride goes on over the course’s still, with a notice under it.

Structure

  1. 1. Ibiza FlyoverBefore a ride in Ibiza only. Nine seconds from the grid to the finish line, while the course loads. Skip flyover or Cancel at any time.ibiza-flyover/default
  2. 2. Countdown“3, 2, 1, Go!” inside the box. The clock starts at 0:00 on “Go!”.class-ride/countdown-ibiza
  3. 3. The classThe plan sets the target resistance for each of 33 segments; the rider just pedals. Pause, Leave, Full screen and Music are always in reach.class-ride/ibiza
  4. 4. Finish bannerAt 20:00, for 3.2 seconds: “Finish!” and the output. The target resistance eases off; the stepper is disabled; Pause and Leave are gone.class-ride/finish-ibiza
  5. 5. Roll-outThe video or the course carries on for a few seconds. Resistance 4 of 32.class-ride/rolling-on-ibiza
  6. 6. Class DoneOpens on its own: the output, the facts, the 17 efforts. Done returns to The Class.class-done/default

States

StateWhat changesMock
PausedThe clock, the bike’s plan and the video or course stop; “Paused” over the box; the button reads Resume.class-ride/paused, paused-ibiza
Off targetThe power hint shows the difference in the warning colour.class-ride/off-target
Lost the bikeAn alert covers the screen under the header; Reconnect.class-ride/disconnected, ibiza-flyover/bike-lost
Short of somethingThe video cannot play, the course cannot be drawn, the bike’s resistance cannot be set: a notice under the box or the panel, and the ride goes on.class-ride/video-unavailable-by-hand, ibiza-unavailable
Course loading“Loading Ibiza Sunset Strip” over the still; the bike already follows the class.class-ride/ibiza-loading

Responsive behaviour

  • In a landscape window from 768px Class Ride does not scroll at any step; the box takes the height the header and the bike panel leave.
  • The countdown, the finish banner and the roll-out lie inside the box at every size, above the bike panel and its stepper. They never cover the header.
  • Ibiza Flyover is three rows that fill the window; it never scrolls.

Accessibility

  • Each moment speaks once through a role="status": “The class starts in 3 seconds”, “Go”, “Finished. 351 kJ.”, “Easy spin. Your summary is coming up.” The pictures of the moment (lights, numeral, band) are hidden from assistive technology.
  • No moment takes focus or blocks the header: the rider can always leave.
  • With reduced motion the flyover is skipped, the countdown and the banner fade, and the roll-out card simply appears.
  • Nothing is said by colour alone: the lights have the numeral, the banner has its words.

Content

  • Use the product’s words: flyover, countdown, finish banner, roll-out, output, efforts.
  • The ride is the rider’s own: no “1st”, “position”, “lap”, “gap” or other riders’ names.
  • The flyover’s status says only what is true of the bike.
