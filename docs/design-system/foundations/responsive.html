Foundation

Responsive

Mobile first, in landscape beside the bike and in portrait in the hand. Breakpoints at 640, 768, 1024 and 1280px, and one rule about height.

Overview

The rider’s device is a tablet or a low-power laptop beside the bike, usually in landscape; a phone is the fallback. Base styles are the phone’s; min-width queries in rem add room. Custom properties cannot be read inside @media, so the widths are repeated there: 40rem, 48rem, 64rem, 80rem.

Height

Two rules read the window’s height, because a landscape tablet is short. Class Ride’s no-scroll layout applies only in (orientation: landscape). The Class draws its side column tighter in a window under 800px tall (1024 × 768), and scrolls rather than cut anything off in one shorter still.

Touch

  • Every target is at least 44 × 44px (--target-comfortable); a shorter control extends its hit area.
  • Anything pressed mid-ride is 48px (--target-ride): the stepper’s buttons, and Pause and Leave in Class Ride’s header on a phone.
  • Nothing depends on hover: there are no tooltips and no hover-only controls.
