Foundation

Typography

Inter Tight for the interface, Barlow Condensed for numerals and the italic display line, JetBrains Mono for identifiers. All three are bundled and self-hosted.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Three families, each with one job. Inter Tight sets every word of the interface. Barlow Condensed sets the numbers a rider reads at arm’s length (power, cadence, the clock, the output) with tabular numerals, and, in black italic upper case, the display lines: the wordmark, the class’s title, “FINISH!”. JetBrains Mono sets the bike’s identifier. Fonts are loaded from assets/fonts/, never a CDN.

Usage

  • Numbers that change while riding use tabular numerals (font-feature-settings: 'tnum'), so they do not shift.
  • Sentence case everywhere. Upper case is the display face’s alone: the wordmark, titles in --text-display, “FINISH!”.
  • A number always has its unit, smaller and quieter, on its baseline: “233 W”, “351 kJ”.
  • Body text keeps a measure of at most --layout-measure.

Accessibility

Sizes are in rem, so the browser’s font size setting scales everything. Text can be zoomed to 200% without loss (1.4.4); line height is at least 1.5 for body text; nothing depends on italic or weight alone.
