Iconography

Lucide line icons, inlined as SVG: a 24-unit grid, a 2px stroke, the colour of the text beside them.

Overview

Icons are Lucide outlines, copied into the markup as inline SVG so nothing is fetched. They take currentColor and one of the size classes. The app uses 24 icons; the sheet below is all of them, taken from the mocks.

12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32 and 40px. Buttons and header controls use 16px; the stepper’s plus and minus and the blocker’s icon 20px; the lost-bike alert 32px.

Usage

  • An icon beside a label is decoration: aria-hidden="true".
  • An icon alone is a button with an aria-label: “Full screen”, “Leave class”, “Cancel”.
  • An icon that carries a state repeats it in words or in shape: the struck-through note for music off, with aria-pressed="false".
  • A turning icon (.icon--spin) always has words beside it.
  • The mark in the lockup is not an icon; see Lockup.

Accessibility

Icons that carry meaning hold 3:1 against their ground (1.4.11): the warning triangle in --color-warn-fg, the lost-bike mark in --color-heart-fg, the connected mark in --color-volt-fg.
