Foundation

Elevation

Surfaces are told apart by fill, not by shadow. Shadows are kept for what floats; z-order has seven named layers.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

On a dark page a shadow hardly shows, so depth is drawn with the three surface fills: page, panel, raised. No screen of the rider’s app casts a shadow. The shadows remain for things that float over the page: the diagnostic recording’s dialog and the core set’s menu, tooltip and toast.

Z-order

Layers are named, never numbered in a component. On Class Ride the header sits one above the overlay layer, so Leave and Full screen stay in reach over the lost-bike alert. The countdown, the finish banner and the roll-out are positioned inside the video’s or the course’s box and need no page-level layer.

Usage

  • Raise by fill first: panel on page, raised on panel.
  • Use a shadow only for something that can overlap content it does not belong to.
  • Over a picture, use a scrim, not a shadow: see Color.
