Foundation

Motion

Quick, quiet motion that says what changed. A ride has three moments of its own: the countdown’s step, the finish banner’s slide and the roll-out card’s rise.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

  • Durations and easings

  • Usage
  • Reduced motion

Overview

Motion is feedback, not show. Hover fills fade in 100ms; things that enter take 150ms; nothing loops except a spinner. The three moments that shape a ride are the only choreography, and each has a reduced-motion form.

Usage

  • Animate opacity, translate and scale only.
  • Entering uses --ease-enter; state changes use --ease-standard.
  • Numbers that tick (power, cadence, the clock) change in place with no animation.
  • Nothing mid-ride moves to draw the eye away from the bike’s numbers.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the tokens zero --duration-fast, --duration-base, --duration-slow, --duration-loop and --duration-pulse, so every token-driven transition and loop stops at once. The ride’s moments swap to the fade forms above, and Ibiza Flyover is skipped (L2-179 AC9). No motion carries information that words do not also carry.
