Foundation

Shape

Soft corners that grow with the surface, hairline borders, and full radius for pills, dots and bars.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Corners scale with what they round: 8px on buttons, 12px on the bike panel’s cells and the video’s box on Class Ride, 16px on the video’s box on The Class and on the roll-out card. Borders are hairlines in --color-line; the only thicker line is the ring of a radio mark.

Usage

RadiusUsed by
--radius-xs, --radius-smSwatches in the plan, small marks.
--radius-mdHeader links, icon buttons.
--radius-lgButtons, Ride with options, step buttons, the countdown’s line.
--radius-xlBike panel cells, the video’s and the course’s box on Class Ride, large buttons, the blocker.
--radius-2xlThe video’s box on The Class, the roll-out card, the diagnostic dialog.
--radius-fullProgress bars, the countdown’s lights, radio marks.

A nested corner is smaller than the corner around it. The finish banner has no radius: it runs off both edges of its box.
