Content

Plain, short, in the rider’s words. The product has a small vocabulary and uses it without synonyms.

Overview

The rider is on a bike. Every string is read at a glance, so it is short, says one thing, and uses the same word for the same thing every time. The words below are the specs’ words; code, docs and the interface share them.

Vocabulary

SayMeaningNot
Telo Ride 1The product. The wordmark is TELO RIDE 1.Telo Ride One, Telo Ride I
the classIntervals to the Beat, the one class.workout, session, video (the video is one way to ride it)
resistanceThe level the bike reports, 1 to 32.gear, difficulty, level alone

effortsThe class’s 17 hard intervals.drops, intervals, sprints (five of them are sprints)

finish bannerThe banner at 20:00.podium, result card
roll-outThe seconds after the finish banner.cool-down (that is a segment of the plan), outro

Connect Bike, The Class, Ibiza Flyover, Class Ride, Class DoneThe five screens.Home, Library, Results

Voice

  • Direct. “Pedal once to wake it up.”
  • Calm when something is short. Say what is lost, then what still works: “The video can’t play. The bike will still follow the class.”
  • The rider’s own number. “Class done. That’s your number to beat.” No ranks, no other riders.
  • No exclamation marks except “Go!” and “Finish!”.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything; upper case only where the display face sets it.
  • Buttons are a verb, or a verb and its object: “Start class”, “Skip flyover”, “Try again”, “Done”.
  • Numbers carry units with a space: “351 kJ”, “233 W”, “91 rpm”; a time is “20:00”, a short one “0:30”; resistance is “13 of 32”.
  • Approximate with “~”: “~254 W average”. Join facts with “ · ”.
  • Typographic apostrophes and quotes: “can’t”.

Error and status formulas

KindFormulaExample
BlockedWhat to do. Why, and what still works.“Open Telo Ride 1 in Chrome or Edge. Bluetooth to the bike only works there — you can look around, but not ride.”

Lost the bikeWhat happened. What is paused and what to do.“Lost the bike. The video is paused. Pedal to wake the bike, then reconnect.”
Not savedWhat failed. Where the result still is.“This ride could not be saved on this device. Your result is above.”
